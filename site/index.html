FOR CLAUDE CODE & CODEX · OPEN SOURCE

Read Qur’an
while your agent thinks.

Send a prompt and a quiet reader opens at the exact ayah you left. When your agent needs you, it steps aside. When the turn ends, your place is saved.

Free. Open source. No account. No tracking.

01 · PROMPT

You hand off a task

The reader opens where you stopped, the moment you send a prompt.

02 · READ

Your agent works

Read at your pace. Every ayah you move is saved locally, instantly.

03 · NEEDS YOU

It asks permission

The reader pauses and points you back to the terminal.

04 · DONE

The turn ends

Your place is kept. One line records the ayat you read, nothing more.

Exact text, never touched

Tanzil’s verified Uthmani text ships byte-for-byte and is checked by SHA-256 before it renders. No model ever writes a letter of it.

Fully offline

Text, fonts and reader live on your machine. Turn off Wi-Fi and it keeps working.

Yours, in plain files

Your position and reading log are small JSON files in ~/.quran-turn. No account, no sync, no analytics.
